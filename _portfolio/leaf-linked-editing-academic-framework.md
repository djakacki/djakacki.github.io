Site lead for the LEAF platform within LINCS. LEAF Commons brings together LEAF-Writer, the Dynamic Table of Contexts, and related tools so that editors can produce semantically rich, linked digital editions without leaving the browser. Recent work extends the platform toward event modeling with TEI and CIDOC-CRM.
