Education
======
* Ph.D. in English Language and Literature, University of Waterloo, 2010
* Marion L. Brittain Postdoctoral Fellow, Georgia Institute of Technology, 2010-2013
* M.A. in English, University of Toronto, 2005
* B.A. in English and History, Lafayette College, 1986

**Dissertation:** *'Covetous to parley with so sweet a frontis-peece': Illustration in
Early Modern Play-Texts.* Supervisor: Dr. Katherine Acheson. Committee: Dr. Jennifer
Roberts-Smith and Dr. Sarah Tolmie. External Reader: Dr. Holger Schott-Syme.

Fellowships and Honours

  Scholar Program: "Early Modern Performance Events and Linked Open Data."
* Marquis Who's Who in America

Research Areas
======
* **Early Modern Studies:** early modern drama and literature, performance,
  performance and cultural history.
* **Digital Humanities:** digital humanities theory and praxis, particularly the
  intersection of textual and spatial analysis, digital pedagogy, visual
  rhetoric, and multimedia theory and design.
