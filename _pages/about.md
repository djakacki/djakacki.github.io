I am Digital Scholarship Coordinator and Associated Faculty in Comparative &
Digital Humanities at Bucknell University, where I have taught since 2014.

My research sits at the meeting point of early modern performance history and
digital scholarly practice. On one side, that means the drama itself: title-page
illustration, touring players, city comedy, and the ways printed playbooks
advertised and interpreted performance. On the other, it means the
infrastructure that makes historical evidence tractable -- text encoding, linked
open data, spatial analysis, and the tools editors actually use.

Much of my current work runs through the [Linked Editing Academic Framework
(LEAF)](/portfolio/leaf-linked-editing-academic-framework/), where I serve as
site lead within the Linked Infrastructure for Networked Cultural Scholarship
consortium, and through [REED London Online](/portfolio/reed-london-online/),
which models performance events and the shifting toponymy of early modern
London. I held a Fulbright Canada Research Chair in Digital Humanities in
2022-23 for work on early modern performance events and linked open data.

I chair the Board of Directors of the Text Encoding Initiative and serve as
Chair-Elect of the Executive Board of the Alliance of Digital Humanities
Organizations. I have been Assistant Director of the Digital Humanities Summer
Institute since 2012.

With Brian Croxall I edit two volumes for the University of Minnesota Press:
*What We Teach When We Teach DH* (2023) and *Teaching Text Encoding*
(forthcoming 2027).
